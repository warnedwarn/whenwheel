# Identity brief

- Mechanism: validators normalize one natural-language availability note into a subset of frozen candidate slots; contract code counts support and selects the earliest maximum.
- Product metaphor: a social clock with times arranged around an orbit.
- Voice: warm, short, coordination-first.
- Interaction: organizer spins up the wheel; each wallet writes one sentence in a persistent bottom dock; the wheel fills with people.
- Result: winning slot illuminates on the circle with unanimous or best-fit state.
- Evidence story: creation, two separately signed availability casts, duplicate-cast rejection, permissionless finalization, stored result.
